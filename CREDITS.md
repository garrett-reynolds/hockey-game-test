Models load from `Assets/Resources/Models/` through `ModelFiles`. Sounds load from `Assets/Resources/Sounds/` through `SoundFiles`. A missing file falls back to the code-made placeholder.

### Notes

- **Puck.** The puck is the model Garrett supplied (above); it replaced the low-poly puck generated for this project (`puck.obj` and `Tools/make_puck.py`, removed). The game scales any puck model to its fixed puck size; a replacement must be flat on its Y axis (Unity up) and named `puck` (one file only).
- **Net and goal light.** The net is the model Garrett supplied (above); it replaced the net generated for this project (`net.obj`, `net.mtl`, and the net half of `Tools/make_net.py`, removed; the rest is now `Tools/make_goal_light.py`). No free (CC0 or CC-BY) goal light could be downloaded without a login, so it was built. The game fits a net model by its `LeftPost`, `RightPost` and `Crossbar` parts: their outer box goes to the posts' outer edges and the crossbar top, from the ice, and the depth runs from the posts' front to the back of the whole model (0.6 m). So a replacement net should have its posts 0.08 m thick, just outside a 1.83 m x 1.22 m opening; a base may flare out past the posts. Name the netting part `Netting` (one readable mesh) so it bulges on a goal; a goal stops on the netting, so its back may slope. Name the goal light's lamp part `Dome` so it lights up. These CC-BY nets can also be downloaded by hand from a Sketchfab account:
  - "Hockey Net" by imveryokay: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hockey-net-04f1b2bbebe4419b8c894c113cf864d9 (CC Attribution)
  - "Hockey goal" by voittohovi: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hockey-goal-42e1d6e7eff54fdf92439638630106d6 (CC Attribution)

- **Goalie character and gear.** No free, login-free rigged hockey goalie was found (checked: Kenney and Quaternius have free rigged humanoids but no goalie; the Sketchfab goalie needs a login and is probably unrigged; Mixamo needs an Adobe login; the Unity Asset Store goalie is paid). So the goalie is assembled from files Garrett supplied: the Quaternius CC0 body, and gear, mask and skates whose source and licence are not known (ask Garrett before any public release). `Tools/make_lifelike_goalie.py` builds one FBX: clothes skinned to the body's rig, gear parented to its bones. The character is the goalie shown by default; the goalie blocks show instead only when the model is missing or the developer flag `forceGoalieBlocks=true` is in the settings file.

- **Boards, glass and arena.** No free (CC0 or CC-BY) boards or glass model could be downloaded without a login, so both the boards and the arena were built instead. The asset research for spec #20 checked Poly Pizza (its stands models are GLB only, which this project cannot import without adding glTFast) and Sketchfab (login needed). The Poly Haven `circus_arena` HDRI (CC0) was considered for the backdrop but not used: the built stands and roof close the arena on their own. If a modelled rink is wanted, this CC-BY model can be downloaded by hand (it would need its glass part named `Glass` and its size matched to `RinkBoards.Box`):
  - "Hockey Rink With UV Map" by rockleyr: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hockey-rink-eeaaf38b50e748feb226a7bdbdcb9138 (CC Attribution)

### Notes

- **Ice.** No free rink-ice model with markings could be downloaded without a login. Most rink diagrams on Wikimedia Commons are CC BY-SA, and the public-domain one (Ice_hockey_layout.svg) is a whole-rink drawing that cannot be lined up with the net, so the markings are drawn by the scripts above from NHL sizes. The markings are a texture rather than separate meshes so that far lines fade smoothly instead of breaking into dots. Delete `ice.obj` to get the plain placeholder ice back.

## Sounds

All 16 are 16-bit PCM WAV, 44.1 kHz, in `Assets/Resources/Sounds/`. The one-shots are normalised to a -3 dBFS peak. Every outside clip was decoded with ffmpeg and then trimmed, mixed, or had its level changed as listed below. Clips marked "made by `Tools/make_sounds.py`" come from this command, run from the repo root:

`python Tools/make_sounds.py Assets/Resources/Sounds --voice ooo.wav --crowd crowd.wav --impact impact`

Its two inputs are decoded first with `ffmpeg -i oooooooooo.ogg -ac 1 -ar 44100 ooo.wav` and `ffmpeg -i crowd_shouting_0.ogg -ar 44100 crowd.wav`. The `impact` folder holds the four Kenney Impact Sounds clips that `make_sounds.py` mixes (named in the Glove catch and Blocker hit rows), each decoded with `ffmpeg -i Audio/<name>.ogg -ac 1 -ar 44100 impact/<name>.wav`.

### Notes

- **Goal horn, buzzer, groan.** No free goal horn, arena buzzer, or crowd groan could be downloaded without a login (Freesound needs an account, and Pixabay blocks scripted downloads). These clips were made instead. To swap in a real recording, replace the WAV with a file of the same name. Keep exactly one file per name, because the game ignores the extension. Then credit the new clip here.
